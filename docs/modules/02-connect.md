# Connecting agents to business systems with just-in-time authorization

**9:45 to 10:30. Owner: Arcade.**

This block runs in the attendee portal at https://arcadeworkshop.dev. Sign in there with your email and the workshop code from your welcome mail; the portal provisions your own Arcade project, identity, loan book and policy, and walks you through the steps below. This page predates the portal and only says where its content went.

## In the portal

- **Your Arcade account**: your own project and project key.
- **Identity**: you join the workshop identity provider with your email and register it in your project. There is no shared Alice and no Bob; the agent acts as you.
- **Join the workshop Slack**: the bot seats you in the room's loan channel.
- **Host the Loan Desk MCP server**: the loan desk tools in your own project, over your own loan book.
- **Start your agent**, **Your first tool use: Slack** and **The Loan Desk's tools**: the agent from `agent/`, authorizing Slack as you the first time a tool needs it, then reading your loan book through its MCP tools.
